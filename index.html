FAQ

Good to know.

Where are you?

Our studio is at 1/71 Evans St, Freshwater NSW 2096. We also offer mobile visits across the Northern Beaches and Mosman to Cremorne.

Do you come to me?

Yes, if you are on the Northern Beaches or between Mosman and Cremorne. Pop your postcode into the checker. If you are outside the mobile area, you are welcome at our Freshwater studio.

What is the founding offer?

During our founding period every full set is 50% off: Classic $95, Hybrid $110, Wispy $125 and Russian volume $135.

How do I choose a style?

Use the look finder for ideas, or simply tell us the look you love. We map every set to your eye shape at your appointment.

How do I look after my lashes?

You will get personal aftercare guidance at the end of your appointment, so your set stays fresh for longer.
